# Survey methodology and reporting commitments

## Purpose

Gather independent feedback about district services, spending priorities, consolidation, leadership, transparency, and school experiences. This is a community feedback instrument, not a legally binding vote.

## Recruitment and eligibility

Participation is voluntary. The survey is intended for adults age 18 or older. Parents, guardians, employees, residents, former students, and other community members may have different perspectives. Published results should describe who participated and how respondents were recruited whenever known.

## Scope and comparison

People with positive, negative, and mixed views are welcome. Some questions are conditional on previous answers and later sections are optional. Nonresponse to a question is not an answer. Survey versions and fielding dates should be disclosed alongside published results. Any historical comparison must explain differences in wording, population, and recruitment method.

## Reporting

- Publish counts of respondents who answered each question and use that question's denominator for percentages.
- Distinguish respondents from stage completions; one respondent completing multiple stages should not count as multiple people.
- Report relevant favorable, unfavorable, neutral, unknown, and skipped responses, not only results supporting a position.
- Label written comments as respondent opinions; review and redact any identifying details before publication.
- Do not claim results statistically represent all residents or voters without a valid probability-sampling design.
- Do not interpret a survey response as proof of misconduct, legal violations, or the motive behind a school-choice decision.

## Public review

The wording in QUESTIONNAIRE.md is a published snapshot for review. It may not yet reflect proposed edits or the live form. Proposed changes should be documented before they are incorporated in live surveys. If wording changes during collection, report the version boundaries and analyze affected responses separately as appropriate.
